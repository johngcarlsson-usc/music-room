How it works

Pick a colour at the bottom. Each colour is an instrument. Draw anywhere, then press play: a line sweeps across the page and plays whatever it touches. Higher on the page is higher notes.

With a pressure pen, pressing harder makes a thicker line, and thicker is louder. Tap a drawing to hear it. Use Move and Erase at the top right to fix things.

On the rainbow "Auto" colour, the shape picks the sound
