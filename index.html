Class Music Room

Four music toys for making beats, drawing songs and moving to music. Tap one to open it, then press play.

Monster Band

A band of monsters plays the beat you tap into the grid: big drum, hi-hat, snare, tom, horn and rattle.

Open Monster Band

Kandinsky Studio

Draw lines and shapes in colour and hear them played back as music. Higher on the page is higher notes.

Open Kandinsky Studio

Hand Band

Wave your hands in front of the camera to touch coloured circles that sing, or play falling-note songs.

Open Hand Band

Class Song Maker

Build a melody and a beat on a grid of coloured squares. Opens with the song the class made.

Open Song Maker

Tips for the classroom

  • Works best in Chrome on a laptop or a classroom screen. Sound starts after you press play.
  • Hand Band needs a webcam: click Allow when Chrome asks to use the camera.
  • Each toy has a full-screen button for showing the class.
  • Settings and beats are remembered on the computer you use, so the class can pick up where it left off.
  • On a Bluetooth speaker, press ] in Monster Band until the monsters line up with the sound, and [ to undo.
